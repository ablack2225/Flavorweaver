Smoky Guajillo-Harissa Chicken over Spicy Tomato-Olive Couscous

Hall of Fame smoky guajillo-harissa chicken thighs over spicy tomato-olive couscous with generous hot honey, smoky cashews, and full-size crispy pepper jack-Parmesan egg-olive egg rolls dipped in hot honey. Original tamarind chutney optional.

Flavor Profile

This complete plate is built around two bold but distinct lanes. The main plate is smoky, tomato-rich, chile-forward, briny, sweet-hot, buttery, and generously sauced. The side event is crispy, cheesy, eggy, spicy and olive-briny, delicious dipped in hot honey; original dark sweet-sour tamarind-date chutney remains an optional alternative.

Target bite: smoky chile chicken + spicy tomato-olive couscous + glossy Thai chili hot honey + sweet-hot cashew crunch + crispy pepper jack-Parmesan egg-olive rolls and hot honey to dip; optional tamarind chutney.

Kitchen Execution Summary

Chicken

Chicken thighs marinated in smoky guajillo-harissa paste with tomato paste, garlic, cumin, coriander, caraway, smoked paprika, red pepper flakes, acid, and salt.

Couscous

Spicy tomato-olive couscous built with 2 1/2 Tbsp reserved paste, tomato paste, spices, chicken broth, green olives, cilantro, and optional olive brine.

Signature Glaze

Generous Thai chili hot honey made with reserved paste, honey, butter, Thai chili, acid, and salt. It should drip into the couscous.

Crunch

Smoky sweet-hot cashews add nutty crunch with smoke, salt, brown sugar, and chile warmth.

Side Event

Full-size egg rolls filled with a 1.5× mixture of soft egg, pepper jack, Parmesan, olives, Thai chili, cilantro and spices.

Showstopper Sauce

Optional original tamarind-date chutney adds a tangy dark-fruity alternate dipping lane.

Measured Ingredients by Component

Smoky Guajillo-Harissa Paste

3 dried guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded; 3 garlic cloves; 2 Tbsp olive oil; 1 Tbsp tomato paste; 1 Tbsp vinegar or lemon juice; optional 1 tsp lemon zest; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tsp coriander; 1/4 tsp caraway; 3/4 tsp smoked paprika; 3/4 tsp red pepper flakes; tiny pinch cinnamon or allspice; 3/4 tsp kosher salt; 1 to 3 Tbsp chile soaking water only as needed.

Chicken Marinade

4 chicken thighs; remaining guajillo-harissa paste after reserving paste for couscous and hot honey; 1 Tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp honey; 1/2 tsp kosher salt; 1/4 tsp black pepper.

Spicy Tomato-Olive Couscous

1 cup plain couscous; 1 Tbsp butter; 1 Tbsp olive oil; 1/4 cup finely diced onion; 2 cloves garlic; 1 1/2 Tbsp tomato paste; 2 1/2 Tbsp guajillo-harissa paste; 1/2 tsp cumin; 1/2 tsp coriander; 1/2 tsp smoked paprika; pinch caraway; tiny pinch cinnamon or allspice; 1 cup chicken broth; 2 to 3 Tbsp chopped green olives; 2 to 3 Tbsp chopped cilantro; optional 1 to 2 tsp olive brine; salt to taste.

Thai Chili Hot Honey Glaze

2 1/2 Tbsp reserved guajillo-harissa paste; 3 Tbsp honey; 1 1/2 Tbsp butter; 1 to 2 finely minced red Thai chiles; 2 tsp vinegar or lemon juice; generous pinch salt; optional pinch cayenne or red pepper flakes.

Smoky Sweet-Hot Cashews

1/2 cup cashews; 1 tsp olive oil or melted butter; 1/2 tsp smoked paprika; 1/4 tsp cumin; 1/4 tsp coriander; 1/8 tsp garlic powder; pinch cayenne or red pepper flakes; 1/4 tsp kosher salt; 1/2 tsp brown sugar.

Crispy Pepper Jack-Parmesan Egg-Olive Egg Rolls

6–8 full-size egg roll wrappers; 3 large eggs; optional 1–2 Tbsp cream or milk; 1/2 cup pepper jack; 1 1/2 Tbsp Parmesan; 1/4 cup chopped green olives; 1 minced Thai chili; 1 1/2 Tbsp chopped cilantro; 3/8 tsp each smoked paprika, cumin and garlic powder; black pepper; salt only if needed; 1 Tbsp flour + 1 Tbsp water sealing paste or beaten egg; frying oil. Fill each with 2–3 Tbsp cooled filling, roll and seal, freeze seam-side down 15–25 minutes, and fry at 350°F about 3–4 minutes.

Optional Original Tamarind-Date Chutney

Tamarind extract: 2 oz / 55 g whole tamarind pods or pulp, about 1/2 cup loosely packed broken tamarind pieces after shells are removed; 1 cup hot water for extraction. Always reduce the strained liquid to 1/2 cup strong extract before making chutney. Chutney: 1/2 cup reduced strong tamarind extract; 2 Tbsp brown sugar; 2 Tbsp honey; 2 Tbsp chopped dates or raisins; 1/2 tsp cumin; 1/2 tsp ginger powder or 1 tsp fresh ginger; 1/4 tsp cayenne; 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes; 1/4 tsp salt; optional tiny pinch garlic powder; 1 tsp rice vinegar, plus 1 tsp more if needed to correct sweetness.

Equipment Map

Electric skillet: cook chicken around 300°F, raising only if browning is too slow.

Oven: roast cashews at 325°F.

Deep fryer or deep pot: fry egg rolls around 350°F.

Small saucepan: warm Thai chili hot honey; prepare tamarind chutney only if serving the optional dip.

Small skillet/pan: soft-scramble eggs and cook couscous base.

Stage-Based Prep Bowls

Paste Bowl

3 soaked guajillos; 3 garlic cloves; 2 Tbsp olive oil; 1 Tbsp tomato paste; 1 Tbsp vinegar or lemon juice; optional 1 tsp zest; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tsp coriander; 1/4 tsp caraway; 3/4 tsp smoked paprika; 3/4 tsp red pepper flakes; tiny pinch cinnamon/allspice; 3/4 tsp salt.

Reserved Paste + Chicken Bowl

Reserve 2 1/2 Tbsp paste for hot honey and 2 1/2 Tbsp paste for couscous. Marinate 4 chicken thighs with remaining paste, 1 Tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp honey, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper.

Couscous Bowl

1 cup couscous; base of 1 Tbsp butter, 1 Tbsp olive oil, 1/4 cup onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 1/2 Tbsp tomato paste, 2 1/2 Tbsp paste, spices, 1 cup broth; finish with 2 to 3 Tbsp olives and 2 to 3 Tbsp cilantro.

Egg-Roll Filling Bowl

3 soft-scrambled eggs, optional 1–2 Tbsp cream/milk, 1/2 cup pepper jack, 1 1/2 Tbsp Parmesan, 1/4 cup chopped olives, 1 minced Thai chile, 1 1/2 Tbsp cilantro, 3/8 tsp each smoked paprika, cumin and garlic powder, black pepper. Cool, portion 2–3 Tbsp per wrapper, roll and seal, and set seam-down in freezer 15–25 minutes.

Cashew Bowl

1/2 cup cashews, 1 tsp oil or melted butter, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/4 tsp cumin, 1/4 tsp coriander, 1/8 tsp garlic powder, pinch cayenne, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp brown sugar.

Optional Chutney Bowl

2 oz / 55 g whole tamarind with 1 cup hot water for extract. Always reduce to 1/2 cup strong extract, then simmer with 2 Tbsp brown sugar, 2 Tbsp honey, 2 Tbsp dates/raisins, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp cayenne, 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1 tsp rice vinegar.

Do Not Forget

Reserve enough paste: 2 1/2 Tbsp for hot honey and 2 1/2 Tbsp for couscous before marinating chicken.

The couscous broth must taste slightly too bold before couscous goes in.

Use cilantro, not parsley, for the final herb lift.

The Thai chili hot honey is the signature sauce. Make it generous.

Use 2–3 Tbsp filling per full-size egg roll; roll, seal, and freezer-set seam-side down for 15–25 minutes.

Cashews burn quickly; roast gently and use brown sugar instead of honey.

If making the optional original chutney, reduce its tamarind extract to 1/2 cup strong extract.

Hot honey is the preferred dip. The optional original chutney needs rice vinegar, cayenne and red pepper flakes to stay tangy-hot.

Full Cooking Timeline and Instructions

  1. Optional — make tamarind extract. Remove shells and tough strings from 2 oz / 55 g whole tamarind pods or pulp. Soak with 1 cup hot water for 15 to 20 minutes, mash thoroughly, then strain and press hard. Pour the strained liquid into a small saucepan and simmer uncovered until reduced to 1/2 cup strong extract. This reduction is canon; it concentrates the sour-fruity tamarind and keeps the chutney from tasting thin or overly sweet.
  2. Toast and soak guajillos. Stem and seed 3 dried guajillo chiles. Toast 10 to 20 seconds per side, then soak in hot water 10 to 15 minutes. Reserve 1 to 3 Tbsp soaking water.
  3. Blend paste. Blend softened chiles with 3 garlic cloves, 2 Tbsp olive oil, 1 Tbsp tomato paste, 1 Tbsp vinegar or lemon juice, optional 1 tsp lemon zest, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1/4 tsp caraway, 3/4 tsp smoked paprika, 3/4 tsp red pepper flakes, tiny pinch cinnamon/allspice, 3/4 tsp salt, and just enough soaking water to make a thick paste.
  4. Reserve paste and marinate chicken. Reserve 2 1/2 Tbsp paste for hot honey and 2 1/2 Tbsp paste for couscous. Coat 4 chicken thighs with remaining paste, 1 Tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp honey, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Marinate at least 1 hour.
  5. Optional — simmer original tamarind chutney. Combine 1/2 cup reduced strong tamarind extract, 2 Tbsp brown sugar, 2 Tbsp honey, 2 Tbsp chopped dates or raisins, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp ginger powder or 1 tsp fresh ginger, 1/4 tsp cayenne, 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes, 1/4 tsp salt, 1 tsp rice vinegar, and optional tiny pinch garlic powder. Simmer 6 to 10 minutes until glossy and spoonable. If it tastes too sweet, correct with another 1 tsp rice vinegar, a pinch of salt, and more cayenne or red pepper flakes.
  6. Roast cashews. Heat oven to 325°F. Toss 1/2 cup cashews with 1 tsp olive oil or melted butter, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/4 tsp cumin, 1/4 tsp coriander, 1/8 tsp garlic powder, a pinch cayenne or red pepper flakes, 1/4 tsp kosher salt, and 1/2 tsp brown sugar. Roast 8 to 12 minutes.
  7. Make egg-roll filling. Soft-scramble 3 eggs until barely set, optionally with 1–2 Tbsp cream or milk. Cool and mix with 1/2 cup pepper jack, 1 1/2 Tbsp Parmesan, 1/4 cup chopped olives, 1 minced Thai chili, 1 1/2 Tbsp cilantro, 3/8 tsp each smoked paprika, cumin and garlic powder, and black pepper. This is the tested 1.5× filling batch; add salt only if needed.
  8. Roll and freezer-set egg rolls. Mix 1 Tbsp flour with 1 Tbsp water for sealing paste, or use beaten egg. Place 2–3 Tbsp filling on each of 6–8 wrappers, fold the lower corner up, tuck the sides in, and roll tightly to a cylinder; seal securely. Freeze seam-side down 15–25 minutes until firm but not frozen solid.
  9. Make couscous base. Heat 1 Tbsp butter and 1 Tbsp olive oil. Add 1/4 cup onion and cook until softened. Add 2 minced garlic cloves, then 1 1/2 Tbsp tomato paste, 2 1/2 Tbsp reserved paste, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, pinch caraway, and tiny pinch cinnamon/allspice. Cook 1 to 2 minutes.
  10. Steam couscous. Add 1 cup chicken broth and bring to a simmer. Taste for boldness. Add salt or 1 to 2 tsp olive brine if needed. Stir in 1 cup couscous, cover, remove from heat, and rest 5 minutes. Fluff, then fold in 2 to 3 Tbsp chopped olives and 2 to 3 Tbsp chopped cilantro.
  11. Fry egg rolls. Fry the firmed rolls at 350°F for about 3–4 minutes until evenly golden, crisp and hot through; drain on a rack.
  12. Cook chicken. Heat electric skillet to about 300°F. Cook chicken until browned and cooked through, about 5 to 7 minutes per side for boneless thighs, longer for bone-in. Rest 5 minutes.
  13. Make hot honey. While chicken rests, warm 2 1/2 Tbsp reserved paste, 3 Tbsp honey, 1 1/2 Tbsp butter, 1 to 2 minced Thai chiles, 2 tsp vinegar or lemon juice, and a generous pinch salt until glossy.
  14. Glaze and plate. Spoon Thai chili hot honey over the rested chicken. Plate spicy tomato-olive couscous, glazed chicken, extra hot honey, smoky sweet-hot cashews, crispy full-size egg rolls and hot honey for dipping; original tamarind chutney optional.

Chef’s Note

Our August 26 live revision replaced smaller triangular briks with full-size egg rolls and a 1.5× filling batch. Use 3 eggs, 1/2 cup pepper jack, 1 1/2 Tbsp Parmesan, 1/4 cup olives, scaled cilantro and spices, and 2–3 Tbsp per wrapper. Roll, seal, freeze seam-down 15–25 minutes, then deep-fry at 350°F for about 3–4 minutes. Dip in the spicy hot honey. Original tamarind chutney remains optional.

Final Taste Target

The finished meal should taste smoky, spicy, tomato-rich, garlicky, briny, buttery, sweet-hot, crisp, cheesy, cilantro-bright, and sweet-hot. The main plate delivers chile chicken, bold couscous, hot honey, and cashew crunch. The side feels like a separate street-food event: crisp wrappers, melty pepper jack, savory Parmesan, soft egg, olives, Thai chili, cilantro, and extra hot honey for dipping, with optional original tamarind chutney.
