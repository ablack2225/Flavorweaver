Brown-Butter Cinnamon-Pecan Marshmallow Sweet Potato Casserole

A fully loaded Southern holiday casserole with deeply roasted sweet potatoes, nutty brown butter, dark brown sugar, maple, warm spice, an intentionally noticeable black-pepper backbone, cinnamon-pecan streusel, and a deeply toasted marshmallow crown.

Kitchen Execution Summary

Whole-Roasted Sweet Potatoes

Roast 3 lb sweet potatoes whole until fully collapsing-soft so the filling stays concentrated rather than waterlogged.

Brown-Butter Filling

Brown butter, dark brown sugar, maple, cream, vanilla, warm spice, salt, and a real black-pepper presence build a plush savory-sweet mash.

Cinnamon-Pecan Streusel

Cinnamon candied pecans, dark brown sugar, flour, butter, and salt create a crisp layer beneath the marshmallows.

Marshmallow Finish

Add marshmallows only after the casserole is hot, then broil hard and fast. Reserved candied pecans go on after broiling so their sugar coating stays fragrant instead of scorched.

Advance Prep

Make-ahead: the filling may be spread into the buttered baking dish up to 24 hours ahead. Keep the streusel in a separate covered container so it stays crumbly.

Final finish: keep marshmallows and the reserved cinnamon pecans separate until the casserole is fully hot.

Cold casserole: if the filling starts refrigerator-cold, add about 10 to 15 minutes to the warming stage before the marshmallows go on.

Service Countdown

2 hours before service: roast whole sweet potatoes until completely collapsing-soft.

75 minutes before service: brown butter; scoop and mash the potatoes; build the filling.

50 minutes before service: spread filling in the buttered 9x13-inch dish and mix the streusel.

35 minutes before service: top with streusel and bake at 350°F until hot and lightly puffed.

8 minutes before service: add marshmallows and broil until deeply golden.

At service: scatter reserved pecans, rest about 10 minutes, and serve warm.

Full Ingredient Roll-Up

Quick-glance ingredient check for the complete casserole. Exact amounts and division points are repeated below.

Roasted Sweet Potato Filling

Sweet potatoes, unsalted butter, dark brown sugar, pure maple syrup, heavy cream, eggs, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, kosher salt, freshly ground black pepper.

Cinnamon-Pecan Streusel

Cinnamon candied pecans, dark brown sugar, all-purpose flour, unsalted butter, kosher salt.

Marshmallow Crown

Mini marshmallows plus reserved cinnamon candied pecans added after broiling.

Measured Ingredients by Component

Roasted Sweet Potato Filling

3 lb orange-fleshed sweet potatoes, scrubbed; 8 Tbsp / 1 stick unsalted butter, browned; 1/3 cup packed dark brown sugar; 2 Tbsp pure maple syrup; 1/3 cup heavy cream; 2 large eggs, lightly beaten; 2 tsp vanilla extract; 1 tsp ground cinnamon; 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg; 1 tsp kosher salt; 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper.

Cinnamon-Pecan Streusel

1 cup cinnamon candied pecans, finely chopped and divided; 1/3 cup packed dark brown sugar; 1/3 cup all-purpose flour; 4 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted; 1/4 tsp kosher salt. Use 3/4 cup pecans in the streusel and reserve 1/4 cup for the finish.

Marshmallow Crown

3 cups mini marshmallows, or enough for a generous nearly full surface layer; reserved 1/4 cup cinnamon candied pecans for the post-broil finish.

Cross-Component Prep Map

Cinnamon candied pecans: 1 cup total; 3/4 cup goes into the streusel and 1/4 cup stays out of the oven until after broiling.

Dark brown sugar: 2/3 cup total; 1/3 cup in the filling and 1/3 cup in the streusel.

Unsalted butter: 12 Tbsp total; 8 Tbsp browned for the filling and 4 Tbsp melted for the streusel.

Salt: 1 1/4 tsp total; 1 tsp in the filling and 1/4 tsp in the streusel.

Black pepper: the full 1 tsp stays in the filling. It should be unmistakable beneath the sweetness.

Stage-Based Prep Bowls

Roast Setup

Sheet Pan

Foil-lined rimmed sheet pan fitted with a wire rack.

Potatoes

3 lb sweet potatoes, scrubbed, dried, and pierced several times.

Filling Bowls

Bowl 1 — Sweetener / Dairy

1/3 cup dark brown sugar; 2 Tbsp maple syrup; 1/3 cup heavy cream; 2 tsp vanilla.

Bowl 2 — Seasoning

1 tsp cinnamon; 1/4 tsp nutmeg; 1 tsp kosher salt; 1 tsp black pepper.

Bowl 3 — Eggs

2 large eggs, lightly beaten. Add only after the mash is no longer piping hot.

Saucepan — Brown Butter

8 Tbsp unsalted butter, browned until amber and nutty.

Topping Bowls

Bowl 1 — Streusel

3/4 cup chopped cinnamon candied pecans; 1/3 cup dark brown sugar; 1/3 cup AP flour; 1/4 tsp kosher salt; 4 Tbsp melted butter.

Bowl 2 — Marshmallows

3 cups mini marshmallows, held until the casserole is fully hot.

Bowl 3 — Final Pecans

1/4 cup cinnamon candied pecans, reserved until after broiling.

Do Not Forget

Roast the sweet potatoes whole so the flesh stays concentrated rather than waterlogged.

Use a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan for even airflow in a non-convection oven and easy cleanup.

Take the potatoes to fully collapsing-soft, not merely fork-tender.

Taste the seasoned filling before adding the eggs. The black pepper should clearly register and the salt should make the sweet potato taste brighter.

Keep the cinnamon candied pecans out of the broiler. Their sugar coating can scorch quickly.

Broil the marshmallows under constant watch. Deep bronze with a few dark edges is the target.

Full Cooking Timeline and Instructions

  1. Roast the sweet potatoes whole. Heat the oven to 400°F with a rack in the center. Set a wire rack over a foil-lined rimmed sheet pan. Scrub and dry 3 lb sweet potatoes, pierce each several times with a fork, and place them on the rack. Roast until completely soft, visibly slumped, and beginning to release sticky syrup, about 55 to 75 minutes depending on size.
  2. Rest and scoop. Let the potatoes cool 10 to 15 minutes, just until safe to handle. Split them and scoop the flesh into a large mixing bowl; discard the skins.
  3. Brown the butter. Melt 8 Tbsp unsalted butter over medium heat. Continue cooking, swirling often, until the milk solids turn amber and the butter smells deeply nutty. Remove from heat immediately.
  4. Build the seasoned mash. Mash the warm sweet-potato flesh until mostly smooth. Add the browned butter, 1/3 cup dark brown sugar, 2 Tbsp maple syrup, 1/3 cup heavy cream, 2 tsp vanilla, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, 1 tsp kosher salt, and 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper. Mix until silky.
  5. Taste before the eggs. The filling should be richly sweet-potato-forward and buttery, with an unmistakable peppery finish and enough salt to make the sweetness pop. Adjust salt or pepper now if needed.
  6. Add the eggs. Let the mash stand about 5 minutes if still piping hot. Whisk in 2 lightly beaten eggs until fully incorporated without beating aggressively.
  7. Fill the dish. Reduce the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9x13-inch baking dish and spread the filling evenly into it.
  8. Make the streusel. Finely chop 1 cup cinnamon candied pecans and reserve 1/4 cup. Mix the remaining 3/4 cup pecans with 1/3 cup dark brown sugar, 1/3 cup all-purpose flour, and 1/4 tsp kosher salt. Stir in 4 Tbsp melted butter until damp, irregular crumbs form.
  9. Bake the casserole. Scatter the streusel evenly over the filling. Bake at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes, until the center is hot, the edges are lightly puffed, and the streusel smells toasted. If refrigerator-cold, cover loosely with foil for the first 20 minutes and expect roughly 35 to 45 minutes total.
  10. Add the marshmallow crown. Remove the casserole and switch the oven to broil. Scatter 3 cups mini marshmallows generously across the surface, leaving a little streusel visible.
  11. Broil hard and watch constantly. Broil just until the marshmallows are swollen and deeply golden with a few dark toasted edges, usually 1 to 3 minutes. Do not walk away.
  12. Finish with cinnamon pecans. Immediately scatter the reserved 1/4 cup cinnamon candied pecans over the hot marshmallow crown.
  13. Rest and serve. Let the casserole stand 10 minutes before serving so the filling settles and the streusel firms slightly. Serve warm.

Final Taste Target

The first bite should read as unmistakable Thanksgiving sweet potato casserole: silky, buttery, sweet, cinnamon-warm, pecan-rich, and marshmallow-toasted. Brown-butter depth, concentrated roasted sweet potato, a clear black-pepper presence, and enough salt should sharpen the sweetness and keep the casserole from becoming cloying. The topping should move from crisp cinnamon-pecan streusel to soft bronzed marshmallow with fresh crunchy pecans on top.

Chef's Notes

The cinnamon candied pecans already bring sugar and spice, so keep the additional cinnamon restrained and let the pecans carry part of the warm-spice character.

Use enough freshly ground black pepper to create a clear warm-savory finish beneath the sweetness.

Roasting the sweet potatoes whole keeps the filling concentrated and avoids excess water.

Troubleshooting

IssueFix
Filling tastes flat before eggsAdd another small pinch kosher salt and a few grinds black pepper. The pepper should be noticeable.
Filling is too sweetAdd 1 to 2 Tbsp heavy cream plus a pinch of salt; do not add more sugar.
Streusel is sandyAdd melted butter 1 tsp at a time until small damp clumps form.
Streusel browns too fastTent loosely with foil until the filling is fully hot.
Marshmallows are paleBroil briefly rather than extending the casserole bake.
Candied pecans are burningKeep the reserved finishing pecans out of the broiler and add them only after the marshmallows are browned.
