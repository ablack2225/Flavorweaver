Charred Korean Corn-Cheese Croquettes

A generous chef-level batch built with frozen sweet corn: hard-charred kernels folded into a concentrated corn-cream panade with precisely cut scallion, jalapeño, garlic, and mozzarella; formed around molten mozzarella cores; armored in panko; deep-fried in clean brisket tallow; and finished with restrained brown honey-butter and smoky sesame salt.

Critical Quantity Map

3 cups thawed, dried frozen sweet corn total = 2 cups for hard charring + 1 cup for the corn-cream puree.

The 2 cups used for charring are cooked in two separate 1-cup batches so the corn browns instead of steaming.

Kitchen Execution Summary

Generous Side Batch

Eight large croquettes support a 3- to 4-piece serving per person when this is the only side and still allow possible leftovers.

Frozen Corn, Properly Treated

Thaw completely, measure after thawing, press dry, air-dry, then divide 2 cups for charring and 1 cup for puree.

Exact Knife Work

Scallion whites are cut in 1/16-inch rounds and jalapeño in 1/8-inch brunoise so flavor disperses without weakening the shell.

Double Cheese Experience

Four ounces of short mozzarella shreds run through the panade while eight measured ½-ounce batons create molten cores.

Brisket-Tallow Production Fry

Run a two-piece test batch, then fry 3 and 3 only when the fryer recovers above 335°F within 30 seconds.

Measured-Bowl Standard

Every prep bowl below includes every ingredient and exact quantity needed at that stage; no searching back through the recipe.

Before Cooking / Advance Prep

Frozen corn: thaw enough to yield 3 measured cups after thawing; drain 5 minutes, towel-press, and air-dry uncovered for 10 minutes.

Knife work: scallion whites/light greens at 1/16 inch; jalapeño at 1/8-inch brunoise; garlic grated to paste; scallion tops fine bias-cut.

Mozzarella: 4 oz short fine shreds plus eight 1¾-by-⅜-by-⅜-inch batons, about ½ oz each.

Deep fryer: use clean filtered brisket tallow filled only to the manufacturer’s proper line and stabilize at 340°F.

No premade Korean sauce: it adds water and duplicates the hoagie’s sauced layers.

Measured Ingredients by Component

Charred Corn-Cheese Panade

3 cups / about 480-500 g frozen sweet corn, thawed completely and dried; 2 tsp neutral oil, divided; 2 Tbsp unsalted butter; ½ cup heavy cream; ½ cup water; ¼ cup all-purpose flour; 2 Tbsp mayonnaise; 2 tsp sugar; 2 green onions; 2 Tbsp jalapeño; 1 tsp garlic paste; ½ tsp fine salt; ½ tsp black pepper; ½ tsp MSG; 8 oz low-moisture mozzarella, divided into 4 oz short shreds and eight ½-oz batons.

Crisp Panko Shell

½ cup all-purpose flour; ½ cup cornstarch; ½ tsp fine salt; ½ tsp garlic powder; 2 large eggs; 2 Tbsp water; 2 cups panko.

Brisket-Tallow Deep Fry

Clean filtered rendered brisket tallow, free of browned solids and water, in the amount required to reach the deep fryer’s proper fill line.

Brown Honey-Butter

2 Tbsp unsalted butter; 1 Tbsp honey; ⅛ tsp fine salt.

Smoky Sesame Salt

2 tsp toasted sesame seeds; ½ tsp fine salt; ¼ tsp smoked paprika; ⅛ tsp MSG; optional 1/16 tsp cayenne; reserved bias-cut scallion tops.

Exact Prep and Cut Standards

Frozen corn: drain, press firmly between towels without crushing, then air-dry on a clean tray for 10 minutes.

Green onions: white/light-green sections in 1/16-inch rounds; dark tops in 1/16- to 1/8-inch bias-cut ribbons.

Jalapeño: 1/8-inch strips, then 1/8-inch dice. Remove ribs and seeds for medium heat or retain some for more heat.

Garlic: grate to a smooth paste.

Mozzarella shreds: strands no longer than about 1 inch.

Mozzarella cores: eight batons, each about 1¾ inches long by ⅜ inch square and approximately ½ oz.

Fully Measured Stage-Based Prep Bowls

Every card is complete and ready to add at its named stage.

Corn Charring and Corn Cream

Char Batch A

1 cup thawed, exceptionally dry frozen sweet corn + 1 tsp neutral oil.

Char Batch B

1 cup thawed, exceptionally dry frozen sweet corn + 1 tsp neutral oil.

Blender — Corn Cream

1 cup corn + ½ cup heavy cream + ½ cup water.

Panade Base and Flavor Finish

Saucepan — Flour Base

2 Tbsp unsalted butter + ¼ cup all-purpose flour.

Aromatic Bowl

2 Tbsp mayonnaise + 2 tsp sugar + white/light-green sections of 2 scallions + 2 Tbsp jalapeño brunoise + 1 tsp garlic paste + ½ tsp salt + ½ tsp black pepper + ½ tsp MSG.

Cheese Bowl

4 oz short finely shredded mozzarella plus eight ½-oz mozzarella batons, kept separate.

Breading Station

Pan 1 — Dry Dredge

½ cup flour + ½ cup cornstarch + ½ tsp salt + ½ tsp garlic powder.

Pan 2 — Egg Wash

2 large eggs + 2 Tbsp water, whisked completely smooth.

Pan 3 — Panko

2 cups panko in a broad shallow layer.

Fryer and Service Finish

Deep Fryer

Clean filtered brisket tallow filled to the proper line and stabilized at 340°F.

Finishing-Salt Bowl

2 tsp sesame + ½ tsp salt + ¼ tsp smoked paprika + ⅛ tsp MSG plus optional 1/16 tsp cayenne.

Brown Honey-Butter Pan

2 Tbsp butter + 1 Tbsp honey + ⅛ tsp salt.

Garnish Cup

Reserved dark-green scallion tops, finely bias-cut.

Do Not Forget

3 cups total means 2 cups charred plus 1 cup pureed.

Frozen corn must be completely thawed and surface-dry.

Cook the panade until it pulls cleanly from the saucepan.

Keep at least ¼ inch of panade around every mozzarella core.

Complete both structural chills.

Run a two-piece test batch before increasing the fryer load.

Full Cooking Timeline and Instructions

  1. Thaw, measure, and divide the corn. Prepare 3 cups dry thawed corn. Divide exactly into 2 cups for charring and 1 cup for puree.
  2. Complete the knife work. Cut the white/light-green portions of 2 scallions into 1/16-inch rounds; cut 2 Tbsp jalapeño into 1/8-inch brunoise; grate garlic to yield 1 tsp paste; prepare 4 oz short mozzarella shreds and eight ½-oz batons.
  3. Hard-char Batch A. Cook 1 cup corn with 1 tsp oil in a hot broad layer until blistered and no longer steaming. Transfer to a tray.
  4. Hard-char Batch B. Repeat with the second 1 cup corn and 1 tsp oil.
  5. Blend the corn cream. Blend 1 cup corn, ½ cup heavy cream, and ½ cup water for 20-30 seconds.
  6. Cook the flour base. Cook 2 Tbsp butter and ¼ cup flour for 90 seconds to 2 minutes without browning.
  7. Build the panade. Add the corn cream in three additions and cook 5-7 minutes until glossy and pulling cleanly from the pan.
  8. Add the entire aromatic bowl. Fold in every measured aromatic ingredient, then fold in all 2 cups charred corn.
  9. Cool and add shredded mozzarella. Cool 8-10 minutes, then fold in 4 oz shredded mozzarella.
  10. Rapid-chill. Spread ½ inch thick and refrigerate 60-75 minutes or freeze 30-40 minutes.
  11. Portion and form. Divide into 8 equal portions, surround each baton with at least ¼ inch panade, and shape into compact 3-inch ovals.
  12. First structural chill. Freeze 20 minutes and repair any cracks.
  13. Bread. Use the complete measured dredge, egg wash, and panko pans in that order.
  14. Second structural chill. Refrigerate 20-30 minutes until the coating feels dry and firmly attached.
  15. Prepare the fryer. Stabilize clean brisket tallow at 340°F and preheat a rack in a 225°F oven.
  16. Run the test batch. Fry 2 croquettes for 4-5 minutes. Confirm the tallow remains 335-345°F.
  17. Complete production batches. Fry 3 and 3 only when the fryer recovers properly; otherwise continue 2 at a time.
  18. Hold uncovered. Rack in the 225°F oven for no more than 12 minutes.
  19. Finish. Brown the measured butter, add honey and salt, mix the measured sesame salt, and brush only the upper crust of each croquette.
  20. Serve generously. Serve 3-4 croquettes per person while the shell is rigid and the core is molten.

Leftovers and Make-Ahead

Cooked leftovers: cool uncovered, refrigerate airtight up to 2 days, and reheat on a rack at 375°F for 8-12 minutes. Do not microwave.

Unfried reserve: tray-freeze breaded croquettes, bag airtight, and fry from frozen at 330-335°F for 6-8 minutes.

Final Taste Target

A rigid, deeply golden panko shell should give way to creamy concentrated corn, blistered kernels, nearly invisible scallion and jalapeño, and a molten mozzarella core. Brown honey-butter should register as quiet nutty sweetness followed by toasted sesame, smoke, salt, and restrained chile.

Chef's Note

This is Korean inspired rather than a traditional Korean preparation. The measured-bowl system is part of the recipe standard: every named bowl must be complete enough to use without hunting through another section.

The eight-piece batch is intentionally generous because these croquettes are serving as the only side beside the hoagies.
