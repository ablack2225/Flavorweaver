Charred Korean Corn-Cheese Croquettes

Hall of Fame corn-cheese croquettes with hard-charred kernels, a thick uncharred corn cream, mozzarella throughout, a molten cheese center, rigid panko crust, brisket-tallow crispness, brown honey-butter, and smoky sesame salt.

Total2-2½ hr
Yield8 large croquettes
Deep FryBrisket tallow · 340°F

Live Verdict

Don: “Best appetizer I’ve ever had.” He also referred to them as “crack.”

Flavorweaver status: Hall of Fame—locked after the first live execution.

Kitchen Execution Summary

Corn Equation

3 cups total = 2 cups charred + 1 cup blended uncharred.

Why Two Char Batches?

One cup at a time prevents crowding and steaming, allowing the kernels to blister.

Critical Cheese Step

Cool the panade 8-10 minutes, then fold in 4 oz shredded mozzarella before spreading and chilling.

Molten Centers

Keep eight separate ½-oz mozzarella batons refrigerated until shaping.

Three Temperature Stages

Chill the completed panade, chill after shaping, and chill after breading.

Fry Standard

340°F tallow; test two first; fry 4-5 minutes until rigid and deeply golden.

Advance Prep

The panade may refrigerate airtight up to 24 hours.

Fully breaded croquettes may refrigerate up to 8 hours.

Fully breaded croquettes may be frozen and fried from frozen at 330-335°F for 6-8 minutes.

Do not apply honey-butter until immediately before serving.

Service Countdown

2 hours before: thaw and divide corn; complete cuts; char corn; blend corn cream; cook panade.

75 minutes before: fold in the shredded mozzarella, spread the panade, and begin the first chill.

40 minutes before: portion, fill with mozzarella batons, shape, and freeze 20 minutes.

20 minutes before: bread, begin final chill, and heat the tallow.

At service: fry, rack, brown honey-butter, season, garnish, and serve.

Measured Ingredients by Component

Crisp Panko Shell

½ cup flour; ½ cup cornstarch; ½ tsp salt; ½ tsp garlic powder; 2 eggs; 2 Tbsp water; 2 cups panko.

Brown Honey-Butter

2 Tbsp butter; 1 Tbsp honey; ⅛ tsp salt.

Smoky Sesame Salt

2 tsp toasted sesame; ½ tsp salt; ¼ tsp smoked paprika; ⅛ tsp MSG; optional 1/16 tsp cayenne; reserved scallion tops.

Full Cooking Timeline — One-Pass Kitchen Method

  1. Thaw and divide exactly 3 cups corn. Divide into 2 cups for charring and 1 cup to remain uncharred for blending. Drain the charring corn 5 minutes, press between towels, and air-dry 10 minutes. The blending cup only needs to be thawed, cooled, drained, and lightly blotted. Microwaving that cup to thaw is acceptable, but cool it fully before sealing it in the NutriBullet.
  2. Complete every cut. Cut the white/light-green parts of 2 scallions into 1/16-inch rounds. Reserve the dark tops. Dice 2 Tbsp jalapeño into ⅛-inch cubes, discarding most loose seeds and retaining about half the ribs. Grate 1 tsp garlic paste. Shred 4 oz mozzarella. Cut the other 4 oz into eight ½-oz batons about 1½ inches long and refrigerate them.
  3. Char Batch A. Heat a broad heavy skillet over medium-high to high. Add 1 tsp oil + 1 cup very dry corn. Spread in one layer, leave untouched 60-90 seconds, toss, and cook another 2-4 minutes until blistered and no longer steaming. Spread on a tray.
  4. Char Batch B. Let the skillet return to full heat and repeat with the remaining 1 tsp oil + 1 cup dry corn. Two batches prevent steaming.
  5. Blend the uncharred corn cream. Add 1 cup uncharred corn + ½ cup heavy cream + ¼ cup water to the NutriBullet. Pulse 5-6 times, blend 15-20 seconds, stop and scrape or shake, then blend briefly again. Add reserved water 1 Tbsp at a time only if needed, using no more than ½ cup total. Stop when no whole kernels remain and the mixture coats a spoon and pours slowly.
  6. Cook the flour base. Melt 2 Tbsp butter in a saucepan. Add ¼ cup flour and stir 90 seconds to 2 minutes, keeping it pale.
  7. Build the panade. Add the corn cream in three additions, whisking smooth after each. Cook with a stiff spatula 5-7 minutes until the mixture gathers into one glossy mass and pulls cleanly from the pan.
  8. Add aromatics and charred corn. Remove from heat. Stir in 2 Tbsp mayonnaise, 2 tsp sugar, all scallion whites/light greens, 2 Tbsp jalapeño, 1 tsp garlic, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, and ½ tsp MSG. Fold in the full 2 cups charred corn.
  9. Cool 8-10 minutes. Stir twice to release steam. The panade should remain warm but no longer be visibly steaming.
  10. Add the shredded mozzarella now. Fold in the full 4 oz finely shredded mozzarella until evenly distributed. Do not spread or chill the panade before this cheese is added. Do not add the center batons yet.
  11. Begin the first chill. Spread the completed panade ½ inch thick on a parchment-lined tray. Press plastic directly on the surface. Refrigerate 60-75 minutes or freeze 30-40 minutes until cold and firm but pliable.
  12. Divide into eight portions. Weigh the chilled panade and divide by eight, generally 95-110 g per portion.
  13. Fill and shape. Flatten one portion, center one refrigerated mozzarella baton, seal completely, and form a compact thick round or oval about 2½-3 inches across and ¾-1 inch thick. Maintain at least ¼ inch panade around the cheese. Repeat.
  14. Chill after shaping. Freeze 20 minutes, then inspect and repair any thin seams.
  15. Build the breading station. Pan 1: ½ cup flour + ½ cup cornstarch + ½ tsp salt + ½ tsp garlic powder. Pan 2: 2 eggs + 2 Tbsp water. Pan 3: 2 cups panko.
  16. Bread all eight. Apply a thin complete dredge, coat in egg, allow 3-4 seconds to drip, then press firmly into panko around every edge and seam.
  17. Final chill. Refrigerate 20-30 minutes, uncovered for the first 10, until the crust feels dry and attached.
  18. Prepare the fryer. Stabilize clean filtered brisket tallow at 340°F. Preheat a wire rack in a 225°F oven. Keep all water away from the fryer.
  19. Test two. Lower two croquettes with a spider into the submerged basket. Fry 4-5 minutes until rigid, deeply golden, and crisp. The tallow should remain within 335-345°F.
  20. Complete production batches. Fry the remaining six in batches of 3 and 3 only when the fryer recovers above 335°F within 30 seconds; otherwise fry two at a time. Return to 340°F between batches.
  21. Hold correctly. Keep uncovered on the rack in the 225°F oven no longer than 12 minutes. Never stack, cover, or use paper towels.
  22. Make brown honey-butter. Brown 2 Tbsp butter, remove from heat, and stir in 1 Tbsp honey + ⅛ tsp salt.
  23. Mix smoky sesame salt. Combine 2 tsp sesame + ½ tsp salt + ¼ tsp smoked paprika + ⅛ tsp MSG and optional 1/16 tsp cayenne.
  24. Finish. Brush only the upper crust with about ¼ tsp honey-butter per croquette. Immediately add a restrained pinch of sesame salt and the scallion tops.
  25. Serve immediately. Plate 3-4 per person while the shell is rigid and the center is molten.

Do Not Forget

3 cups corn = 2 cups charred + 1 cup blended uncharred.

Char only one cup at a time.

Begin the NutriBullet with only ¼ cup water.

Fold in 4 oz shredded mozzarella after cooling and before the first chill.

Keep the eight center batons refrigerated until shaping.

Complete the panade chill, shaped chill, and breaded chill.

Rack the fried croquettes; never use paper towels.

Leftovers and Make-Ahead

Cooked leftovers: cool uncovered, refrigerate airtight up to 2 days, and reheat on a rack at 375°F for 8-12 minutes. Do not microwave.

Frozen unfried: tray-freeze fully breaded croquettes, bag airtight, and fry from frozen at 330-335°F for 6-8 minutes.

Apply honey-butter only after frying or reheating.

Troubleshooting

Corn will not brown: it is wet or the skillet is crowded; dry it and char only 1 cup at a time.

NutriBullet will not circulate: add reserved water 1 Tbsp at a time, never exceeding ½ cup total.

Panade too loose: return it to the saucepan and cook longer; never repair with raw flour.

Shredded cheese turns oily: the panade was too hot; cool 8-10 minutes first.

Cheese leaks: the center was off-center, the seam was thin, the croquette was warm, or the tallow was above 345°F.

Crust softens: use a rack, leave uncovered, and brush only the upper crust.

Chef's Note

This is Korean-inspired rather than a traditional Korean preparation. It translates Korean corn-cheese flavor architecture into a sealed croquette with two forms of corn and two forms of mozzarella.

The live test locked both cheese layers: 4 oz shredded mozzarella folded into the cooled panade before chilling and eight separate ½-oz mozzarella batons inserted during shaping.

Testing Note

The original draft failed to place the shredded-mozzarella addition clearly before the first chill. Amy had to remove the panade from the freezer, fold in the cheese, respread it, and restart the chill. That redo was caused by deficient sequencing, not cook error. The canonical recipe now repeats the correct transition in the summary, prep bowls, timeline, and do-not-forget section.
