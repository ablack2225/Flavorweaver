# Loaded Bacon-Shallot Three-Cheese Green Bean Casserole

> Wide Italian-cut green beans in a deeply savory cream-of-mushroom sauce loaded with bacon, butter-Worcestershire mushrooms, shallot, garlic, Velveeta, sharp cheddar, Parmesan, and black pepper, finished with a crisp bacon–fried-onion–Parmesan–panko crown.

## Advance Prep

- Drain beans at least **10 minutes** and blot if wet.
- Drain and blot canned mushrooms thoroughly.
- Bacon can be cooked ahead; reserve about one third for topping.
- The casserole base can be assembled several hours ahead and refrigerated.
- Keep the final topping separate until the casserole is hot.
- If baking refrigerator-cold, allow about **10 extra minutes** before topping.

## Stage-Based Prep Bowls

### Stage 1 — Drain + Bacon
- **Colander 1:** 4 cans Italian-cut green beans, drained at least 10 minutes.
- **Colander 2:** 2 cans sliced mushrooms, drained and blotted dry.
- **Skillet:** 1 lb chopped bacon; cook crisp and reserve 1 Tbsp fat.
- **Bowl 1:** 2/3 cooked bacon for filling.
- **Bowl 2:** 1/3 cooked bacon for topping.

### Stage 2 — Mushroom + Aromatic Pan
- **Pan:** 1 Tbsp bacon fat + 1 Tbsp butter.
- **Bowl 1:** mushrooms.
- **Bowl 2:** 1 tsp Worcestershire + 1/4 tsp black pepper.
- **Bowl 3:** diced shallot + minced garlic.

### Stage 3 — Sauce
- **Bowl 1:** 2 cans cream of mushroom + 1/2 cup milk + 1/4 cup heavy cream.
- **Bowl 2:** 1 1/2 tsp Dijon + 1 tsp Worcestershire + 3/4 tsp black pepper.
- **Bowl 3:** 4 oz Velveeta + 4 oz cheddar + 1/3 cup Parmesan.
- **Bowl 4:** 1/2 cup French fried onions.

### Stage 4 — Topping
- **Bowl 1:** 1/3 cup panko + 1/4 cup Parmesan + 1 Tbsp melted butter or bacon fat.
- **Bowl 2:** 1 1/2 cups French fried onions + reserved crisp bacon.

## Do Not Forget

- Drain canned beans and mushrooms **very well**.
- Brown mushrooms **before** shallot enters the pan.
- Add mushroom Worcestershire only after browning and cook until it glazes the mushrooms.
- The sauce should be slightly looser than final casserole consistency before the beans go in.
- Melt Velveeta gently; add cheddar and Parmesan over low heat.
- Do not automatically add salt. Bacon, soup, Worcestershire, cheeses, and fried onions are already salty.
- Canned beans are already cooked. The oven is for heating, melding, and crisping.
- Keep the final topping out until the last **8 to 10 minutes**.

## Full Cooking Timeline and Instructions

1. **Drain thoroughly.** Drain **4 cans Italian-cut green beans** for at least 10 minutes. Drain **2 cans sliced mushrooms** separately and blot mushrooms dry. Blot beans if visibly wet.

2. **Cook the bacon.** Cook **1 lb chopped thick-cut bacon** over medium heat until deeply browned and crisp. Transfer to towels. Leave **1 Tbsp bacon fat** in the skillet. Divide bacon roughly **two-thirds for filling and one-third for topping**.

3. **Brown the mushrooms.** Add **1 Tbsp butter** to the bacon fat over medium-high heat. Add dry mushrooms in a broad layer and cook until their moisture is gone and edges take real color, about **5 to 8 minutes**.

4. **Glaze the mushrooms.** Add **1 tsp Worcestershire** and **1/4 tsp black pepper**. Toss **30 to 60 seconds**, until glazed and nearly dry.

5. **Cook the aromatics.** Reduce heat to medium. Add **1 large diced shallot** and cook **2 to 3 minutes**. Add **3 minced garlic cloves** and cook **30 seconds**.

6. **Build the sauce.** Reduce heat to medium-low. Stir in **2 cans cream of mushroom soup**, **1/2 cup milk**, **1/4 cup heavy cream**, **1 1/2 tsp Dijon**, **1 tsp Worcestershire**, and **3/4 tsp black pepper** until smooth and steaming.

7. **Melt the cheeses.** Add **4 oz Velveeta** and stir until melted. Lower heat and add **4 oz extra-sharp cheddar** and **1/3 cup Parmesan**. Stir just until smooth; do not boil.

8. **Adjust the sauce.** It should coat a spoon but still flow. If too thick, add **2 Tbsp milk** at a time, up to **4 Tbsp**. Taste before adding any salt.

9. **Fold together.** Off heat, gently fold in the drained beans, **two-thirds of the bacon**, and **1/2 cup French fried onions**.

10. **Bake.** Heat oven to **350°F**. Lightly butter a **9x13-inch dish**, add casserole, and bake uncovered **18 to 22 minutes**, until bubbling and fully hot. From refrigerator-cold, allow about **28 to 32 minutes**.

11. **Build the topping.** Toss **1/3 cup panko**, **1/4 cup Parmesan**, and **1 Tbsp melted butter or bacon fat**.

12. **Crown the casserole.** Scatter over **1 1/2 cups French fried onions**, the panko-Parmesan mixture, and the reserved crisp bacon.

13. **Finish.** Bake another **8 to 10 minutes**, until deeply golden and crisp.

14. **Rest and serve.** Rest **5 minutes**. The sauce should cling, bacon should be obvious, and the topping should stay crunchy.

## Final Taste Target

The casserole should remain unmistakably green bean casserole—cream of mushroom and fried onions are still recognizable—but every layer should be amplified. The wide beans should be coated in silky mushroom-cheese sauce; the canned mushrooms should taste browned and Worcestershire-rich; shallot and garlic should deepen the savory base; bacon should show up in nearly every bite; black pepper should be clear; and the top should deliver crisp bacon, Parmesan, panko, and fried-onion crunch.

## Troubleshooting / Rescue Notes

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sauce too thick before beans | Add milk 2 Tbsp at a time |
| Sauce too loose | Simmer gently 2 to 3 minutes before cheese |
| Casserole watery | Drain/blot canned vegetables longer next time |
| Mushrooms taste canned | Brown longer before Worcestershire |
| Sauce looks greasy | Remove from heat and whisk in 1 to 2 Tbsp milk |
| Flavor feels flat | Add pepper first, then a few drops Worcestershire; salt last |
| Topping soft | Add only for final 8 to 10 minutes |

## Chef's Notes

The canned ingredients are treated deliberately. Wide Italian-cut beans give the sauce more surface area, while canned mushrooms are transformed separately in bacon fat, butter, Worcestershire, and black pepper before they meet the condensed soup.

Velveeta supplies sauce stability and silkiness; extra-sharp cheddar supplies the main cheese flavor; Parmesan adds nutty savory depth. A full pound of bacon makes bacon part of the casserole rather than a garnish.
