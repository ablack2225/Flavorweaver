Deep-Roasted Chicken Mole Enmoladas

Juicy shredded chicken thighs wrapped in homemade corn tortillas and coated in a deep roasted mole built from Doña María concentrate, guajillo, charred produce, sesame, nuts, three saltine crackers, and restrained 60% bittersweet chocolate. Live-tested and corrected for a one-pass kitchen workflow.

Critical Live-Test Corrections

Sesame: Toast all 2 Tbsp at once, then immediately divide 1 Tbsp for the mole and 1 Tbsp for garnish.

Onion: Cut 1/4 cup white onion into paper-thin half-moons, about 1/16 inch thick.

Sour-cream crema: Whisk 1/4 cup sour cream with 2 tsp cold water. Add more water only 1/2 tsp at a time.

Mole consistency: It must run from the whisk in a thick ribbon and lose whisk trails within about 2 seconds. If it holds tracks like paste, add warm broth in 1/4-cup additions.

Service: Dip, fill, roll, sauce, garnish, and serve. Do not bake.

Kitchen Execution Summary

Chicken and Broth

Simmer 1 pound chicken thighs with onion, garlic, bay, salt, and pepper. Pull at 180–190°F and reserve at least 3 cups broth.

Roasted Mole Foundation

Toast guajillo; char tomato, onion, and garlic; toast 2 Tbsp sesame once and divide; toast nuts and exactly 3 saltines.

Mole Finish

Fry the strained puree, fry in Doña María paste, loosen with broth, simmer, add 14 g bittersweet chocolate, then correct to dipping consistency.

Near-Service Assembly

Coat chicken, prepare onion and crema, condition tortillas, dip in mole, fill, roll, garnish, and carry directly to the table.

Service Countdown

90 minutes before serving: Begin the chicken and start the roasted mole foundation.

60 minutes before serving: Blend, strain, fry the puree, add Doña María, and begin the simmer.

30 minutes before serving: Add chocolate, correct mole consistency, and coat the chicken.

10 minutes before serving: Slice onion, thin sour cream, set out reserved sesame and cilantro, then condition tortillas.

At service: Dip, fill, roll, sauce, garnish, and serve immediately.

Measured Ingredients by Component

Chicken and Broth

1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, 3 average or 4 small; 3 cups water; 1/4 medium onion; 2 crushed garlic cloves; 1 bay leaf; 1/2 tsp fine salt; 1/2 tsp black peppercorns or 1/4 tsp ground pepper.

Roasted Mole Foundation

1 dried guajillo, stemmed and seeded; 1 Roma tomato, halved; 1/4 medium onion, wedges; 2 skin-on garlic cloves; 2 Tbsp sesame seeds, toasted together and divided 1 Tbsp + 1 Tbsp; 2 Tbsp roasted unsalted peanuts or almonds; 3 plain saltine crackers; 1/2 chipotle in adobo; 1/4 tsp cinnamon; tiny pinch clove; 1 cup warm broth.

Mole Concentrate and Finish

1 Tbsp neutral oil; 4 oz Doña María mole paste; 1 1/2 cups warm broth for initial loosening; up to 1/2 cup additional warm broth for dipping consistency; 1/2 oz / 14 g Ghirardelli 60% bittersweet chips; fine salt; up to 2 tsp honey or brown sugar only if needed; up to 1/2 tsp cider vinegar only if needed.

Chicken, Tortillas, and Fresh Finish

1 3/4 cups shredded chicken; 1/2 cup finished mole; 7 homemade corn tortillas; 2–3 Tbsp neutral oil; 1–1 1/2 cups hot mole; 1/4 cup paper-thin white-onion half-moons; 1 Tbsp reserved toasted sesame; 1/4 cup sour cream + 2 tsp cold water; 2 Tbsp cilantro.

Cross-Component Prep Map

Broth: Keep at least 3 cups available after straining. Use 1 cup in the blender, 1 1/2 cups after the paste, and reserve up to 1/2 cup for final consistency.

Sesame: Toast 2 Tbsp once. Label 1 Tbsp MOLE and 1 Tbsp GARNISH immediately.

Chicken: Measure 1 3/4 cups after shredding and reserve any extra before adding mole.

Mole: Protect 1/2 cup for chicken and 1–1 1/2 cups for dipping and final saucing.

Crema: Complete the 1/4 cup sour cream + 2 tsp cold water mixture before tortilla service begins.

Stage-Based Prep Bowls

Bowl 1 — Roast and Toast

Roma tomato, onion wedges, skin-on garlic, 2 Tbsp sesame, 2 Tbsp nuts, and 3 saltine crackers.

Bowl 2 — Blender

Softened guajillo, roasted produce, 1 Tbsp toasted sesame labeled MOLE, nuts, crackers, chipotle, cinnamon, clove, and 1 cup broth.

Bowl 3 — Mole Finish

Oil, Doña María paste, 1 1/2 cups broth, up to 1/2 cup correction broth, chocolate, salt, and optional sweet-acid corrections.

Bowl 4 — Table Finish

Paper-thin onion half-moons, 1 Tbsp sesame labeled GARNISH, 1/4 cup sour cream whisked with 2 tsp cold water, and cilantro.

Full Cooking Timeline and Instructions

  1. Cook the chicken. Combine 1 lb thighs, 3 cups water, 1/4 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 bay leaf, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp peppercorns. Simmer gently, partially covered, for 22–30 minutes, until 180–190°F and easy to pull apart.
  2. Rest and shred. Rest 10 minutes. Strain and keep at least 3 cups broth. Measure 1 3/4 cups shredded chicken.
  3. Toast and soak guajillo. Toast 10–15 seconds per side, soak in very hot water for 15 minutes, then drain.
  4. Roast produce. Dry-roast the tomato, onion wedges, and skin-on garlic for 8–12 minutes. Peel garlic.
  5. Toast all sesame once. Toast 2 Tbsp sesame over medium-low, stirring constantly, about 30–60 seconds. Divide immediately: 1 Tbsp MOLE + 1 Tbsp GARNISH.
  6. Toast nuts and crackers. Toast 2 Tbsp nuts for 1–2 minutes. Toast 3 saltines for 30–45 seconds per side without scorching.
  7. Blend and strain. Blend softened guajillo, roasted produce, 1 Tbsp sesame labeled MOLE, nuts, crackers, 1/2 chipotle, cinnamon, clove, and 1 cup broth for 1–2 minutes. Strain.
  8. Fry the puree. Heat 1 Tbsp oil; cook the strained puree 8–10 minutes until dark and concentrated.
  9. Add Doña María. Fry in 4 oz mole paste for 2–3 minutes.
  10. Loosen and simmer. Whisk in 1 1/2 cups warm broth. Simmer partially covered over low heat for 25–30 minutes.
  11. Add chocolate late. During the final 10–15 minutes, whisk in 1/2 oz / 14 g 60% bittersweet chips.
  12. Correct dipping consistency. Whisk trails must disappear in about 2 seconds. If the mole holds tracks like paste, add warm broth in 1/4-cup additions, simmering 1–2 minutes after each.
  13. Balance. Salt first. Add honey or brown sugar only for a hard bitter edge. Add cider vinegar only by drops if the seasoned sauce remains heavy.
  14. Coat chicken. Combine 1 3/4 cups chicken + 1/2 cup mole. Add broth only if needed.
  15. Prepare garnish. Slice 1/4 cup onion into 1/16-inch half-moons. Whisk 1/4 cup sour cream + 2 tsp cold water. Set out the reserved sesame and cilantro.
  16. Condition tortillas. Warm each tortilla in a thin film of oil for 5–8 seconds per side. Keep covered.
  17. Dip, fill, and roll. Dip each tortilla in hot mole, add 1/4 cup chicken, roll, and place seam-side down.
  18. Sauce and garnish. Add 1–1 1/2 cups mole, paper-thin onion, reserved toasted sesame, sour-cream crema, and cilantro. Serve immediately.

Troubleshooting

Mole is paste-thick: Add warm broth in 1/4-cup additions; simmer 1–2 minutes between additions.

Mole tastes raw: Simmer 10–15 minutes longer.

Mole is bitter: Confirm salt first, then add 1 tsp honey or brown sugar and simmer before retasting.

Tortillas tear: Thin the mole, rewarm tortillas, and make sure the oil pass was sufficient.

Crema is too thick: Add cold water 1/2 tsp at a time.
